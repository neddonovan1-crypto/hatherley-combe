# Hatherley Combe: persons, places and devices wanting art

Compiled 27 November 2026 from the game as it stands across the open pull requests: the engine and creation choices (#8), the rewritten events (#9), the Steward's Book page (#10) and the economy proposal (#12). Where an item exists only in the #12 proposal it is marked *proposed*.

**How to read the descriptions.** The game's prose almost never says what anybody looks like. Where the text does fix a detail (an age, a coat, a bitten footman) it is given as fact. Everything else in a description is a suggestion for the artist, drawn from the person's part in the story, and may be overruled.

**The squire himself** is addressed as "you" throughout and has no face in the text. Recommended: never show him full-face. A hand with a pen, a back at a window, a shadow on the stackyard wall. If a portrait is wanted for the title page, it needs five variants to match the "past" choices (old soldier, post-captain, courtier, farmer bred, stranger).

Counts: 13 principal persons, 49 lesser persons or groups, 6 animals, 44 places, about 95 devices.

## 3. Devices (icons)

Small, one-colour, in the manner of printer's ornaments and ledger marks.

### 3a. The four accounts (always on the page)

| Device | Meaning | Suggested emblem |
|---|---|---|
| Purse | Ready money | A netted purse |
| Mortgage | Debt on the estate | A sealed parchment with a ribbon |
| Standing | With the county | A cocked hat, or a card of invitation |
| Goodwill | With the parish | A cottage door standing open |
| Household | The family's temper | A hearth |

Also wanted: wheat price (a bushel measure), harvest and weather (sun, rain, frost), rental (a rent-roll), a rise and a fall mark, "living on credit" (a tradesman's bill on a spike).

### 3b. Seasons and quarter days

Spring / Lady Day, Summer / Midsummer, Autumn / Michaelmas (a goose), Winter / Christmas. Four head-pieces.

### 3c. The thumb tabs of the Steward's Book

Accounts, Works, Persons, Wheat, Day-Book. Plus the handbill frame for public events (royal arms, a pointing hand) and a wafer seal for private letters.

### 3d. Regard (how a person stands towards you)

A five-step mark from "will not forget this" to "won over". Suggested: a hat, from jammed on to swept off.
